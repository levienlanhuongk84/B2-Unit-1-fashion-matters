Hoạt động 1 · Quần áo và vẻ ngoài trong đời làm nghề của bạn

3:00Nhìn 6 ảnh street style, mỗi ảnh trả lời 3 câu hỏi tiếng Anh bên dưới.
Ba người bạn, ba phong cách
  1. Câu 1
    "Look at the three people. How would you describe each person's style in one or two words?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    The woman on the left has a minimalist, elegant style — a cream suit and trainers. The man in the middle has a streetwear look — an oversized hoodie, cargo trousers and chunky boots. The woman on the right has an eclectic, colourful style with bold patterns.

  2. Câu 2
    "Which of the three styles is closest to your own everyday style? Why?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    I'd say the woman in cream — I prefer a simple, put-together look I can wear to work rather than very bold prints or an oversized streetwear look.

  3. Câu 3
    "Do your own friends dress similarly to each other, or very differently, like this group?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    My friends are actually quite different from each other too — some are casual, some dress more formally — and I don't think that matters at all.

Blazer rộng phối cùng jeans
  1. Câu 1
    "She has mixed a smart blazer with casual jeans and trainers. What do we usually call this kind of mix?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    It's often called a smart-casual mix — combining a tailored piece like a blazer with relaxed items such as jeans and trainers.

  2. Câu 2
    "Would you wear an outfit like this to work? What about to meet friends?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    Not to work — in a hospital I need a white coat over smart, comfortable clothes — but it would be perfect for meeting friends or going out at the weekend.

  3. Câu 3
    "What single item do you think makes the biggest difference to how smart she looks?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    The blazer — without it, this would just be a plain T-shirt and jeans, but the blazer instantly makes the outfit look put-together.

Trong một cửa hàng quần áo nhỏ
  1. Câu 1
    "Where do you think this woman is, and what is she doing?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    She's in a small boutique or vintage shop, browsing through a rail of clothes, probably deciding what to try on.

  2. Câu 2
    "Do you enjoy shopping for clothes in person, or do you prefer shopping online? Why?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    I prefer shopping online because it saves time, but I do enjoy browsing in small shops occasionally — you can touch the fabric and try things on properly.

  3. Câu 3
    "Her outfit mixes several patterns and layers. Do you think mixing patterns like this looks stylish or messy?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    It can look stylish if it's done carefully, using one or two colours to tie the patterns together — but it's easy to get wrong and end up looking messy.

Một buổi tiệc tối sang trọng
  1. Câu 1
    "What kind of event do you think this is, and how can you tell from their clothes?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    It looks like an evening party — she's wearing a long silk dress and heels, and he's in a suit with no tie, which suggests a smart but not too formal evening event.

  2. Câu 2
    "How is her outfit different from something she might wear to work or at the weekend?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    It's much more formal and glamorous — the satin fabric, the slit skirt and the heels are designed to stand out at an evening event, not for everyday wear.

  3. Câu 3
    "Do you have an outfit like this for special occasions? When did you last wear it?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    I have one 'go-to' outfit for weddings and parties — I last wore it a few months ago at a colleague's wedding.

Trên đường đến phòng gym
  1. Câu 1
    "What clues in the picture tell you this woman is on her way to, or from, the gym?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    She's wearing a sports bra and leggings, carrying a gym bag and a water bottle, and there's a gym visible through the window behind her.

  2. Câu 2
    "Some people wear sportswear all day, not just for exercise. What do you think about that?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    I think it's fine for running errands or travelling because it's comfortable, but I probably wouldn't wear leggings and a sports bra to work or to a restaurant.

  3. Câu 3
    "How often do you exercise, and what do you usually wear when you do?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    I try to exercise three times a week, and I usually just wear a T-shirt, shorts and trainers — nothing as fashionable as this outfit!

Trên đường tới văn phòng
  1. Câu 1
    "What job or industry do you imagine this man works in, based on his clothes?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    He looks like he works in finance, law or business — a dark suit, a tie and a leather briefcase are typical of a corporate office job.

  2. Câu 2
    "In your own workplace, is this kind of formal dress required, optional, or never worn?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    In my hospital, doctors don't wear suits — we wear a white coat over smart, comfortable clothes; a suit and tie would be impractical.

  3. Câu 3
    "Do you think the way someone dresses at work affects how colleagues or patients see them?"
    Gợi ý trả lời

    Yes, I think it does — smart, neat clothing can make a good first impression and suggest professionalism, even if it doesn't affect someone's actual skill.

Sáu quy tắc
📏-ER / MORE
  • Tính từ 1 âm tiết thêm -er / -est.
  • Tính từ 2 âm tiết trở lên dùng more / the most.
  • Chính tả — tận cùng -e: chỉ thêm -r / -st.
  • Chính tả — nguyên âm + phụ âm: gấp đôi phụ âm.
  • Chính tả — phụ âm + y: đổi thành -ier / -iest.
  • old → older → the oldest
  • nice → nicer → the nicest
  • big → bigger · slim → slimmer
  • dirty → dirtier · skinny → the skinniest
  • casual → more casual → the most casual
🔁HAI DẠNG ĐỀU ĐÚNG
  • Một số tính từ 2 âm tiết dùng được cả -er/-est lẫn more/most.
  • simple → simpler / more simple
  • common · likely · narrow
  • pleasant · polite · gentle
⚠️BẤT QUY TẮC
  • Không thêm -er.
  • Không thêm more.
  • good → better → the best
  • bad → worse → the worst
  • far → farther / further → the farthest / furthest
  • old → older / elder → the oldest / eldest
📉LESS / THE LEAST · NOT AS … AS
  • less … than và the least … là chiều ngược của more / the most.
  • not as … as = không … bằng (not so … as hiếm gặp hơn).
  • less serious than · less expensive than
  • the least achievable figure
  • Sally is not as tall as her brother.
  • These shoes don't look as comfortable as the flip flops.
🎚️ADVERBS OF DEGREE
  • Trước comparative: a bit · slightly · a little · a lot · much · far · rather · a great deal.
  • Trước superlative: by far · easily · much · quite.
  • He is much older than me.
  • your feet are a lot bigger than mine
  • slightly better value for money
  • by far the most creative student
  • easily the biggest animal in the world
🏃SO SÁNH TRẠNG TỪ
  • Trạng từ đuôi -ly: more / less + trạng từ.
  • Trạng từ ngắn thêm -er.
  • Một số trạng từ bất quy tắc.
  • more commonly · more readily available
  • less seriously · less exclusively
  • well → better · badly → worse
  • fast → faster · hard → harder
Dùng với
  • than sau comparative: She is two years older than me.
  • the trước superlative: It was the happiest day of my life.

Mẫu A · dress code

Out of the three options, I'd go for scrubs for everyone, and I'll explain why. The main reason is hygiene. Scrubs are loose-fitting, they're washed by the hospital every day, and they're by far the most practical option when you're on your feet for twelve hours. They're also much cheaper for staff than a wardrobe of smart clothes, and nobody has to weigh up the options every morning — you just put them on.

Admittedly, a white coat presents a more professional image, and many patients still expect it. However, coats are worn for days, so they're nowhere near as clean as scrubs. Smart everyday clothes with a name badge look friendly and let people express their personality, but they're the least practical choice: a silk blouse or a good pair of trousers is ruined the first time a patient is sick on it, and staff would end up spending a fortune on clothes.

On balance, I've decided on scrubs because they're twice as practical as the other two options and only slightly less smart. The best option would be to give everyone two sets in the same colour, so the team looks well-dressed and, more importantly, safe.

Mẫu B · đám cưới

Given the choice, I'd advise her to go for the third option — wear something she already has and add new accessories. It's a lot cheaper than the other two, and a stylish necklace or a new pair of shoes can change the look of an outfit completely. She could splash out on one good item instead of a whole outfit — in my experience, people notice accessories far more than the dress itself.

Admittedly, buying online is convenient and there's a huge choice. However, it's the riskiest option: the dress might not fit, it might be badly made, and there's no time to take it back before the wedding. Renting a designer dress sounds elegant, and it's far less expensive than buying one, but she'd worry all evening about spilling something on it — nowhere near as relaxing as wearing her own clothes.

All things considered, the third option is the best value for money: she'd look presentable, she wouldn't spend a fortune, and she'd feel comfortable as well as stylish. If she really wants something new, the best way to do it is to save up and buy one well-made dress after the wedding, when the sales start.

Mẫu

If I were you, I'd go for a plain shirt and dark trousers rather than jeans — it looks far more professional. A well-made jacket is much smarter than a hoodie, and it's easily the safest choice for a scholarship interview. Comfortable shoes matter too, because you don't want to look scruffy after a long journey. On balance, smart-casual is a good compromise — you don't need a full suit, but you should still dress for the occasion.
